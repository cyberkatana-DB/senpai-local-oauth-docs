Senpai Local

Senpai Local is private personal automation operated by its owner. It helps produce a private daily digest on systems the owner controls.

There is no public product signup, no shared online service for other users, and no third-party dashboard for browsing mail.

What it does

With the owner's permission, Senpai Local may read Gmail message metadata and content solely to prepare that owner's private local daily digest. Retrieved data stays on the owner's own systems.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell data.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking pixels.
  • It does not share mailbox contents with third parties.
  • It does not provide human review of mail by default.

Access and control

Google account access is granted through Google's OAuth consent screen. Tokens remain on the owner's local systems. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

For questions about this private automation, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for Senpai Local.
