Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This policy describes how Senpai Local handles information. Senpai Local is private personal automation operated by its owner. It is not a public multi-user service and does not offer public account signup.

Information processed

When authorized by the owner through Google OAuth, Senpai Local may read Gmail message metadata and content solely to produce the owner's private local daily digest.

Retrieved mail data is stored locally on systems the owner controls. OAuth tokens also remain on those local systems.

Purpose of processing

The only intended purpose is preparing the owner's private local daily digest. Senpai Local is not operated to profile other people, sell insights, or build advertising audiences.

Sharing

Senpai Local does not sell personal data, does not serve ads, does not use analytics or tracking technologies on these pages, and does not share mailbox contents with third parties for their own purposes. There is no routine human review of mail contents.

Google provides the OAuth authorization flow. Google's own privacy terms apply to Google's services separately from this policy.

Retention and control

Locally stored digest materials and tokens remain under the owner's control. The owner can delete local files at any time and can revoke Google access through Google Account permissions.

These website pages

This homepage and privacy policy are static informational pages. They do not set cookies, do not run tracking scripts, do not collect form submissions, and do not load third-party assets.

Contact

For privacy questions about Senpai Local, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this application.
